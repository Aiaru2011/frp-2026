# Presentation Coach
### Your Personal AI Presentation Trainer

## About the Project
Presentation Coach is an AI-powered website that helps people improve their presentation skills and become confident public speakers.

Users practise speaking in front of their webcam and receive feedback on their speaking pace, eye contact, long pauses, and filler words.

Our goal is to help people communicate naturally and confidently without reading every word from their slides.

## The Problem
Many students feel nervous when speaking in public. They may speak too quickly, use filler words, pause for too long, or struggle to maintain eye contact.

They need an easy way to practise and receive useful feedback.

## Our Solution
Presentation Coach provides a personal training experience that helps users understand their speaking habits and improve through repeated practice.

## Key Features
- Speaking pace analysis (words per minute)
- Eye contact estimation
- Face-in-frame detection
- Long pause detection
- Filler word detection
- Personalised practice feedback
- Repeated practice to improve performance

## How It Works
1. The user starts a practice session.
2. The user presents in front of their webcam.
3. The system analyses speech and visual information.
4. A practice summary displays the results.
5. The user receives suggestions for improvement.
6. The user practises again and compares their performance.

## Our Vision
We want to make presentation training accessible to everyone and help people share their ideas clearly, naturally, and confidently.

## Current Status
The website is working, and testing is underway to evaluate its measurements and feedback.
