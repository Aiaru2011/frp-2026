How to practise

  1. Frame your face. Sit or stand so your eyes are level with the camera.
  2. Look at the lens. Glance at the camera at the end of each sentence, not at your own picture.
  3. Keep notes low. Put notes under the camera, so looking at them does not break contact.
  4. Repeat the talk. Two or three short runs beat one long one.
